What the museum is made of

The museum pages live on GitHub Pages at wrhistorymuseum.github.io. The words, stories, and numbers live in the museum's Supabase project, the one this panel signs into. This panel is the admin page of the same site, so keeping the museum current never needs a rebuild: change it here, and the museum reads the change.

Who owns what

Site account: the region's GitHub account, wrhistorymuseum, on the Epistoleus email. Back end: the Supabase project under the DG Premier account. Moving the back end to a role account is the standing Phase 3 step, so a future handoff moves accounts, not the museum.

How to add the next admin

Open the Admins screen, add their name and email, and they are on the list. They sign in with that email, use “Email me a sign-in link instead” on the sign-in screen the first time, then set their own password with “Set or change my password” at the top of the panel. Always keep at least two admins on the list.

What to do each month

Check Submissions and approve, hold, or reject everything waiting. Download a backup from Backup & Restore and keep the file somewhere safe. Glance at Home / Numbers so the region can say how the museum is doing.

If you are locked out

Use “Email me a sign-in link instead” on the sign-in screen. The link signs you in once; set a fresh password right away with “Set or change my password.” If no admin can get in at all, the person who holds the back-end account can add an email back to the admins list from that account.
